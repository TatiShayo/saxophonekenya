# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Last Updated:** October 30, 2026  
**Scope:** SaxophoneKenya (Live Musical Entertainment & Booking Portal)

This Acceptable Use Policy governs the acceptable use of **SaxophoneKenya**. All users must comply with these rules.

---

## 1. Prohibited Uses
You may not use SaxophoneKenya:
1. **Unlawful Activities:** To facilitate, promote, or execute any activity that violates the laws of Kenya or your local jurisdiction.
2. **System Attacks:** To launch denial-of-service attacks, automated port scans, brute-force attacks, or automated vulnerability probes.
3. **Intellectual Property Theft:** To scrape, copy, or redistribute copyrighted data or proprietary algorithms.
4. **Harassment & Hate Speech:** To generate, store, or transmit content that is threatening, defamatory, obscene, or infringing.
5. **Resource Abuse:** To exceed documented API rate limits or computational quotas through multi-account manipulation.

---

## 2. Enforcement & Termination
We reserve the right to monitor system metrics for abuse patterns. Violations of this policy may result in immediate suspension, account termination, and reporting to law enforcement authorities without prior notice.
