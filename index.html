Frederic Sax Ke

Fredrick Ogonda Odundo • Nairobi, Kenya

Fredrick Ogonda Odundo (professionally known as Frederic Sax Ke) is one of Nairobi's most sought-after live saxophonists and luxury event music curators. Specialising in high-society weddings, corporate galas and diplomatic functions, Frederic delivers bespoke acoustic and amplified performances across Kenya and East Africa.

With acclaimed headline appearances at established Nairobi musical residences including Buddies & Grill, Jam & Judge, Bosses & Roses and Mingle City, Frederic crafts sophisticated musical sets ranging from classic jazz standards and neo-soul to contemporary Afrobeats and high-energy celebration anthems.

Beyond solo appearances, Frederic directs turnkey musical production through vetted event partners covering sound reinforcement, live audio engineering, MC hosting, DJ sets and visual documentation — ensuring unified coordination from ceremony to late-night reception.

Visual portfolios, event aesthetics and behind-the-scenes stage journals are curated on Instagram (@ojie_f.red7) and photographic diary (@ojiespov).

Standard Performance Agreement

Business: Frederic Sax Ke • Artist: Fredrick Ogonda Odundo • Email: saxophonekenya@gmail.com

1. Booking confirmation & deposit — Dates are exclusively locked upon receipt of a 50% commitment deposit. The remaining 50% balance is settled on the event day prior to performance start.

2. Technical & sound check — The venue or client provides clean power access. Soundcheck occurs approximately 90–120 minutes prior to guest arrival.

3. Cancellation & rescheduling — Rescheduling requests communicated at least 14 days in advance allow the full deposit to transfer to any mutually open date on the calendar.

4. Performance rider — A minimum performance area of 2m x 2m, dedicated power within 15 metres, and access to the venue sound system or a supplied line-array arrangement.

5. Liability — Frederic Sax Ke maintains public liability insurance for the duration of the engagement. The client retains responsibility for guest conduct and venue safety.
